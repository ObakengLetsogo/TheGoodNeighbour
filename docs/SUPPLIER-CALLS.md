# Supplier and partner calls: scripts, questions and the quote sheet

Purpose: replace every placeholder number in the system with a **real, written price** before any money is spent. Record each answer in
[`quote-sheet.csv`](quote-sheet.csv) (it opens in Excel or Google Sheets; every grocery item in our hampers is already listed).
Then send the numbers to the product team (Claude) and the profit model gets re-run.

## Rules for every call

1. **Be honest about where we are.** We are *planning* a funeral benefit service and gathering prices. We are **not trading**, **not licensed** and
   **have no insurer yet**. Never say or imply otherwise.
2. **Don't promise volumes, exclusivity or a start date.** "If it works we'd need a reliable supplier at a fixed price for 6 to 12 months."
3. **Always ask for the price in writing** (WhatsApp or email is fine) with a **valid-until date**.
4. **Get at least two quotes** for every category, so we can compare.
5. **Never take or give a deposit,** and **sign nothing** until the lawyer has seen it.
6. **Don't share one supplier's price with another.**
7. **Keep TGN completely separate from any employer:** don't use work contacts, work phones or work email, and don't mention or use anything learned at work.
8. **Write it down while you're talking** (the sheet's Notes column), and note the person's name and the date.

## The 20-second opener (use this every time)

> "Hi, my name is ___. I'm putting together a funeral benefit service for families, so I'm gathering prices. It's still at the planning
> stage, so I'm not placing orders yet, but I'd like to understand what you offer and what it costs. Do you have five minutes?"

If they ask who it's for: "Families in the North West, starting small." If they ask about volume: "I can't promise volumes yet. If it works, we'd want a supplier
we can rely on at an agreed price."

## What each call is trying to learn

| Call | The number we need | Compare against our placeholder |
|---|---|---|
| Grocery wholesaler | Price of each hamper item, delivered to a North West town | Hamper cost: R2 500 → R1 950, R5 000 → R3 800, R7 500 → R5 700, R10 000 → R7 500 |
| Livestock supplier | Price per sheep and per cow, delivery, health papers | Sheep R1 500, cow R7 000 |
| Coffin maker | Wholesale price by tier; colour and trim options | (none yet) |
| Undertaker | Price per service; whether a fixed-price partnership works | (none yet) |
| Payment provider | Fee per collection, per failed collection, set-up and monthly fees | R5 per collection (a guess) |
| Insurer or managing agent | Rates by age band, commission, whether in-kind is possible | Placeholder age bands |

## A. Grocery wholesalers (in Johannesburg and in North West towns)

**Ask:**
- [ ] Can you supply the items on our hamper list? (Read the list. Print or send it.) Which can you not supply?
- [ ] Price **per item** and **per bag or pack**, at **10, 50 and 100 hampers' worth**? Is there a bigger discount at higher volumes?
- [ ] Do you **deliver** to Rustenburg, Mahikeng, Klerksdorp, Potchefstroom, Brits (or the town we pick)? **Cost** per delivery? **Lead time**? Can you deliver within **48 hours** of an order?
- [ ] **How long is the price valid?** Can you lock it for 3, 6 or 12 months? How often do prices change, and by how much?
- [ ] **Payment terms:** cash on delivery, or an account? (We will not have credit at first.)
- [ ] Do you supply **disposable plates, cups, cutlery** and **still water** in bulk?
- [ ] Can you **pack** items into hampers, or only supply loose?

**Listen for:** a price that changes every week; refusal to quote in writing; large minimum orders; delivery that takes more than 2 days.

## B. Livestock suppliers (farmers, auction agents, feedlots)

**Ask:**
- [ ] Price per **sheep** and per **cow**. What **weight and age** does that price buy?
- [ ] Can you supply **one animal at a time on 48 hours' notice**, anywhere in the North West? What is your **maximum per month**?
- [ ] Is the **price fixed for 6 to 12 months**, or does it move with the market? By how much does it swing between seasons?
- [ ] **Transport:** do you deliver? Cost per trip or per km? Who arranges the **health certificate** and the **movement permit**, and what do they cost?
- [ ] Can you offer **slaughter and dressing** as a separate service, at what price? (We don't include it in the bundle, but families will ask.)
- [ ] What happens if an animal **dies or is sick** before delivery?
- [ ] What do you need to be **paid**, and when?

**Listen for:** vague "market price" with no ceiling; no way to deliver quickly; no paperwork for moving animals.

## C. Coffin and casket makers

**Ask:**
- [ ] What models do you make? Please show photos and prices. Which would you put in an **Essential**, a **Dignity** and a **Heritage** tier?
- [ ] **Wholesale price** at **10, 50 and 100** units? Minimum order?
- [ ] **Lead time** for stock and for a rush order? Can you **deliver** to a North West town? Cost?
- [ ] Can you **change colour or a small detail** on the same model? At what cost?
- [ ] Would you hold a **fixed price for 6 to 12 months**? What would make that price change?
- [ ] **Quality and warranty:** what are the materials and the finish? Anything we should check?
- [ ] Do you already supply funeral parlours? Would you supply a **non-parlour** buyer?

## D. Undertakers (funeral service providers)

**Ask:**
- [ ] What does a **standard funeral service** cost, and what does it include (collection, mortuary, hearse, paperwork, service)? What is **extra**?
- [ ] **Home-village transport** from Johannesburg to a North West town: price per km or per trip?
- [ ] Would you work at a **fixed, published price per tier** if we sent you families? What would you need to make that work?
- [ ] **How are you paid today** by insurers? How long does it take?
- [ ] Are you **registered** (FSP or otherwise) for the funeral services you sell? (Ask politely; we need partners who are in good standing.)
- [ ] What do you do if a family asks for **more than the agreed price**? (Our promise is no top-ups, so we need to know how this works.)

**Listen for:** unclear or changing prices; pushing families to spend more; unlicensed insurance-like products.

## E. Our own delivery (van, fuel, driver)

- [ ] Quote for hiring a **bakkie or small truck** with a driver for a trip from Johannesburg to a North West town and back.
- [ ] Cost of **fuel and tolls** for the round trip, and how long it takes.
- [ ] Is it cheaper to **buy locally** in the North West and deliver within the town? (Ask local wholesalers and farmers.)

## F. Payment provider (Stitch or an alternative)

**Ask:**
- [ ] **Fee per successful collection**, **per failed collection**, **per mandate set-up**. Any monthly or minimum fee?
- [ ] **Volume tiers:** what does the price become at 500, 2 000 and 10 000 collections a month?
- [ ] **Settlement time**, and **failed-collection retries**: how many, when, and what do they cost?
- [ ] **Several people paying one plan:** each needs their own debit order. Any extra cost per mandate?
- [ ] What do you need from us to **start** (documents, registered company, bank account)? Can we talk before we are registered?
- [ ] **Contract term**, **exit terms**, and any **minimum commitment**.
- [ ] Do you offer anything **cheaper than a debit order** for small amounts (e.g. pay-by-bank)?
- [ ] Alternatives: please ask for two other providers' names.

**Why it matters:** at an assumed R5 per collection, a R50 premium loses about 10%. The minimum contributor share (default R50) is set from this answer.

## G. Insurer or managing agent

Go with the one-page pitch (the strategy document, section 1 and 6) and these questions. **Don't share any employer's information.**

- [ ] Can the benefit be **in kind** (groceries and livestock) under your licence? If not, can it be **cash with an in-kind option**?
- [ ] What does the **cash alternative** have to equal?
- [ ] **Premium rates by age band** for a funeral benefit of about R5 000 to R15 000 per life, and the **claim rates** you assume.
- [ ] Do you work with **distributors** who are not licensed insurers? Under what arrangement (representative, binder, outsourcing)? What **licences** would we need?
- [ ] **Commission or administration fee** to us, and who **collects the premium**.
- [ ] How do you treat a **family plan** where several people pay one policy, and a policyholder who is **not covered** (paying for parents)?
- [ ] **Waiting periods**, **maximum ages**, **lapse and reinstatement** rules you require.
- [ ] **Claims turnaround** you expect, and what documents you need.
- [ ] How long to **onboard**, and what it costs us.

## H. Lawyer (financial services)

Take section 7 of [`STRATEGY.md`](STRATEGY.md) with you. The four questions, in order: (1) can the benefit be in kind and what must cash equal, (2) what authorisation to sell and administer,
(3) premium payer who is not the policyholder and consent of the covered people, (4) what the website, app and any video may say.

## How to judge the answers

- **Supply cost at or below our placeholder** keeps the margin we assumed. **Above it**, the plan's price or the margin has to change. Tell the product team.
- **A price you can't hold for 6 months** is a risk to the plan's promise. Ask for a ceiling or a review rule.
- **If two suppliers differ by more than 20%**, ask the dearer one why, and ask the cheaper one whether they can really deliver.
- **Delivery and failure matter as much as price.** A cheap supplier who is late on a funeral is the most expensive one.

## After each call

1. Fill in the row(s) in `quote-sheet.csv`: supplier, contact, price, minimum, lead time, valid-until, **written quote? (Y/N)**.
2. Send a short thank-you message and ask for the price in writing if you don't have it.
3. When about **10 rows per category** are filled in, open the admin dashboard, go to **Supplier Prices** and upload the sheet. It works out what each hamper, sheep and cow
   really costs us (the lowest price for each part, with prices in writing counted first), flags anything still missing or not in writing, and lets you apply the
   costs to the catalogue and send the payment fee and typical bundle cost to the **Profit Model**. Then send it to the product team and we'll tell you whether the
   plan makes money at those prices, and what to change if it doesn't.
