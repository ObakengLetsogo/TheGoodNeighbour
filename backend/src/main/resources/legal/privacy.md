## 2. What we collect, and why
We only collect what we need to run your plan.

About you: your name, mobile number, email address if you give one, South African ID number, and date of birth. We need these to set up your plan, to check who you are, to work out your price, and to contact you.

About the people on your plan: their name, relationship to you, date of birth and, if they are 16 or older, their ID number. We need these to work out each person's price and to check a claim.

About a claim: the name of the person who passed away, the date and cause of death, the documents you upload (such as a death certificate and ID), and the name, number and address of the person we deliver to.

About funeral wishes: if you write down what a person would like (burial or cremation, place, church, music, who to call first), we keep that so the family doesn't have to guess.

About payments: what was paid, when, by whom and how. We do not store your bank card or full bank account details; those are held by our payment provider.

About family plans: if you invite someone to share the cost, we keep their name and mobile number, and how much they pay.

About how you use the app: messages we send you, support requests you make, and a record of what our staff do on your plan (an audit trail).

## 3. How we use your information
To set up and run your plan, collect payments, work out prices, send you messages about your plan, deliver a bundle when there is a claim, answer support requests, keep our records as the law requires, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Family plans: who sees what
If you share a plan with family, the people paying can see: the plan's price, who pays what share, who has paid this month, the names, ages and relationships of the people covered, and any funeral wishes saved for them.

They cannot see anyone's ID number or mobile number. Only the owner of the plan can see the numbers of the people they invited.

If you are invited to a plan, we only show you the invitation once your mobile number is verified as yours.

## 5. How we protect it
ID numbers and uploaded documents are encrypted when stored. Passwords are stored in a form we cannot read. Only our staff who need it for their work can see your information, and what they look at is recorded. The app and our servers talk to each other over encrypted connections.

No system is perfectly safe. If your information is ever exposed in a way that could harm you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as the law requires.
