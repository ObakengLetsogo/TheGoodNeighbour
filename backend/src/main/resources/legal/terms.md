A person who is invited can accept or decline, and can change their share or leave at any time. Leaving does not cancel the plan; their share goes back to the owner.

We review the claim and tell you our decision. If it is approved, we deliver the bundle on a date we agree with you, and the app shows it. One claim can be made for each covered person.

If you choose the cash alternative [where offered], it is worked out as [describe: for example, what we would have paid to supply the bundle at the time of the claim], and is not more than the value of the bundle.

We may decline a claim, for example if it falls inside a waiting period, if the person was not on the plan, or if information given was false. We will tell you why.

If the person who passed away was the one who owned the plan, the plan carries on for [60] days so the family is not rushed. Within that time, our team can hand the plan over to a family member. After that, the plan ends unless someone has taken it over.

If a claim was made by mistake, tell us. We can reverse it and the person stays covered.
